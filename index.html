Platform

One system, three jobs

Aqua is not a once-a-year training day. It is a small piece of the working week, measured continuously.

Phishing simulation

Scheduled, realistic test emails sent to staff inboxes in a sandboxed simulation. Anyone who clicks lands on a short, supportive explainer — not a scolding — and a two-question check that the lesson landed.

Micro-learning

Modules under five minutes, built for people checking training off between lessons or admin tasks — not a 40-minute course nobody finishes.

Risk-score reporting

One live number per department, exportable for accreditation and audit — a dashboard the college can show, not a spreadsheet someone has to rebuild each term.

How it works

From sign-up to a safer inbox

Four steps, in order — this is the sequence every department follows when they come on board.

01

Onboard staff

Import a department's staff list and set their training frequency.

02

Assign training

Short modules are scheduled and sent out automatically.

03

Run a simulation

A realistic phishing test goes out, safely sandboxed, with no real risk.

04

Track and report

Results land on the dashboard, ready to export for compliance.
